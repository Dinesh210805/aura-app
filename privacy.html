Privacy Policy

Who we are

AURA is an on-device voice assistant that can see your screen and perform actions for you when you ask it to.

What AURA processes

  • Voice — audio you speak is transcribed so AURA can understand commands.
  • Screen content — while running a task, AURA reads on-screen elements to decide what to tap or type. Banking and payment apps are always blocked.
  • Notifications & media (only if you grant access) — read to answer questions and perform actions you request. Banking and security notifications are filtered out.
  • Contacts (only if you grant access) — matched on-device to find a number when you say "message <name>". Numbers never leave your phone.

Where processing happens

Perception and on-device intelligence run locally on your phone. When you configure an AI provider key, the minimum content needed to fulfil a request may be sent to that provider — for example Groq or Google — under their terms. AURA does not run its own analytics or advertising servers.

Keys & secrets

Provider API keys you enter are stored in Android's encrypted preferences on your device, and are sent only to the provider you configured.

Crash reports and usage data

Crash reporting is enabled so faults can be fixed. Usage analytics record only the tool name, how long it took, and whether it succeeded — never task content, screen content, tool arguments, or tool results.

Data we sell

None. AURA does not sell your data.

Your controls

You can revoke any permission in Android Settings at any time, and clear stored memory, learnings and activity logs from within the app.

Terms of Service

Acceptable use

Use AURA only for lawful purposes, and only to automate things you are allowed to do on your own device and accounts. Do not use AURA to bypass security controls or to access accounts that are not yours.

AI can make mistakes

AURA is powered by AI and can misunderstand instructions or act incorrectly. Always review important or irreversible actions. Sensitive actions ask for your confirmation before proceeding.
